The Idea

This started as a question, not a plan. I wanted to know what I could actually build using just my phone — no laptop, nothing fancy. Coding felt fun and exciting from the first line, and I didn't want to stop once I started. I kept questioning how far a phone-only setup could really go, and this calculator became the answer.

What It Does

What started as a basic calculator grew into a full mathematics toolkit. It handles standard arithmetic, scientific functions (trig, logs, powers), and a memory system — but it goes further, into symbolic algebra and calculus (simplify, expand, factor, solve, differentiate, integrate), number theory (primes, GCD, LCM), matrices and vectors, unit conversions, and function graphing with zoom and pan.

Most operations come with "Steps on How" — instead of just showing an answer, it explains the working, so it doubles as a learning tool. There's also a camera-scan feature that reads a handwritten or printed problem and solves it directly.

Currently sitting at over 90% of the full roadmap complete.
