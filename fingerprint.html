Fingerprint

🟢 Live · Experiment
Started Oct 2026 · HTML · CSS · JavaScript

The Idea

Every website you open can read a surprising amount about your device without asking. Fingerprint shows you exactly what that is, then turns it into a uniqueness score and a short fingerprint code. Everything runs inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What It Checks

DeviceScreen size, CPU cores, memory, touch support and platform.
GraphicsA canvas drawing hash and the GPU name reported by WebGL, which are some of the most identifying signals.
Locale and fontsLanguage, timezone and how many common fonts are installed.
Score and hashA 0–100 uniqueness score and a short code built from all the signals together.

What I Learned

Clearing cookies does not make you anonymous. Ordinary settings, taken together, can already make a device stand out.
